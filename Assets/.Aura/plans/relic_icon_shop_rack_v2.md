# Relic Icon, Base-vs-Hovered Numbers, and the Shop Rack

## Objective
Replace the shop's text relic rows with square 1:1 relic icons on a straight rack — price at rest, name and full tooltip on hover — and make relics and cards show printed base numbers at rest and final stat-scaled numbers on hover.

## Changes
- 'Assets/Scripts/Items/RelicDefinition.cs' - add `RelicScaling.Resolve(int statTotal)`; add `EffectFormula(ItemInstance)` for the base-plus-scaling line and an `extraStat` overload of `EffectSummary` for the final number
- 'Assets/Scripts/Cards/CardData.cs' - add a `BuildDescription` overload that prints a supplied value instead of one it calculates
- 'Assets/Scripts/UI/IHoverView.cs' - (create) one-method interface so the hover lift drives a card or a relic icon
- 'Assets/Scripts/Combat/CardHoverEffect.cs' - drive and cache an `IHoverView` instead of a hard `CardView` reference
- 'Assets/Scripts/Combat/CardView.cs' - implement `IHoverView`; render the printed base at rest and the stat-scaled value on hover
- 'Assets/Scripts/UI/CardTooltip.cs' - add a `Show(title, body, source, Component owner)` entry point and a settable clamp `bounds`, so one tooltip serves cards and relics and stays inside the shop frame
- 'Assets/Scripts/UI/RelicText.cs' - (create) the tooltip body for a relic: effect, formula, stat lines
- 'Assets/Scripts/UI/RelicIconView.cs' - (create) one square relic icon: body, caption slot, canvas group, buy click, hover
- 'Assets/Scripts/UI/RelicRack.cs' - (create) the straight rack: lift, spread, leftward bend, and dimming of the icons that are not hovered
- 'Assets/Scripts/UI/ShopPanel.cs' - `relicRows` becomes `RelicIconView[]`; add `FillRelicRack` and `Buy(RelicIconView)`
- 'Assets/Editor/CardGameSceneBuilder.cs' - add `BuildRelicIconPrefab`, `MakeShopRelicSlot`, `MakeShopRelicRow`; wire the new prefab into `BuildRoomScene`; hand the shop frame's `RectTransform` to the tooltip bound
- 'Assets/Prefabs/RelicIcon.prefab' - (create, by the builder) the icon prefab
- 'Assets/Scenes/Room.unity' - regenerated by the builder; not hand-edited

## Relevant Assets and Quirks
- **The icon at rest shows nothing but its price.** The caption under the body is the price while at rest and is swapped for the relic's name on hover, in the same slot and the same text object. The body itself is a flat placeholder square with no text: a sprite will replace it later, and `ItemData`/`RelicDefinition` have no `art` field today, so nothing is authored.
- **The tooltip is the only information surface.** Its body is, in order: the effect line (if the relic has one) carrying the **final** resolved number, then a short formula line, then the relic's stat lines. `RelicInstance.StatLabel` (`STR`/`AGI`/`INT`/`VIT`/`WIL`) is the per-stat label, matching the bag rows.
- **What "base" means.** A relic's formula folds its own stats into the flat number: Loaded Dice (`baseValue 20`, `perStatPoint 1`, guaranteed `agility 1`) reads `(21 + AGI)%`, or `(23 + AGI)%` when the roll also gave +2 Agility. The hovered effect line is only the resolved total, as if the relic were owned. A card at rest shows its printed `baseValue`; hovered, its stat-scaled value.
- **Double-counting guard.** `Inventory.GetBonuses` already folds a carried relic's own stats into `PlayerStats`, so the "add this relic's own stats" figure is only for a relic that is **not owned yet** — the shop rack. `EffectSummary(stats)` keeps its current meaning (defaulted `extraStat = 0`) for the bag and sell views; only the rack passes a value.
- **A relic with `RelicEffect.None`** has no effect line. Its tooltip is the stat lines plus tier and description.
- **Cards with `valueSource != Fixed`** (`PerCardInHand`, `PerCardPlayed`, `EmptyHand`) have no printed base — `baseValue` is the per-card amount and resolving with no context yields 0. They keep their live read in both states. This is the single carve-out to the rest/hover split.
- **Overlap is accepted.** The relic rack sits at panel-x −960…−20 with the equipment rack at +20…+960, so a 430-wide tooltip to the right of an icon covers the equipment rack. The racks are **not** narrowed. The tooltip is clamped to the shop panel's `RectTransform` rather than the screen, so "everything stays in the shop frame", and the non-hovered relics are dimmed so the hovered one reads as the subject. The equipment rows are `ShopEntryView` and are not dimmed; the shop's equipment rack is not part of this change.
- **Dimming must not block input.** The icon dims through a `CanvasGroup`, and `blocksRaycasts` is left true, so a faded icon is still hoverable and clickable and the pointer can move between neighbours.
- **Hover rules that must not be broken**: no `Canvas` with `overrideSorting` on an icon (it sorted cards behind the deck view at orders 100 and 200); no sibling reordering on hover (layout reflow slid the card out from under the pointer and caused jitter). Lift stays scale-only plus direct writes to `anchoredPosition`/`localRotation`; the tooltip stays a single shared instance on its own order-500 canvas with every graphic's `raycastTarget` off.
- **The tooltip is straight by construction**: it is its own canvas object, so the icon's leftward bend cannot rotate it.
- **`CardTooltip.Owner` is today a `CardView`** and `CardView.OnDestroy` hides the tooltip when it still owns it; widening `Owner` to `Component` keeps that guard working for both types.
- **`ShopEntryView` keeps serving the equipment rack.** `BindCard` becomes unused but is left in place; removing it is not part of this change.
- **`CardGameSceneBuilder` is the single source of truth**: the icon prefab, the rack layout and `ShopPanel`'s references must all be authored there, or the next `Build Game Scenes` run reverts them.
- **The editor was in play mode** on `Assets/Scenes/Room.unity` at last check. `BuildGameScenes()` calls `EditorSceneManager.SaveOpenScenes()` and then `NewScene(..., NewSceneMode.Single)`, so play mode must be stopped before the rebuild step.

## Verify
- Console is free of compile errors; `CardHoverEffect` logs no "no IHoverView" warning on the card or relic prefabs.
- In the shop, three relics sit in a straight line with no rows, each a small square with its price underneath and nothing else; no relic body carries text.
- Hovering a relic lifts and enlarges it, parts its neighbours, bends it slightly left, keeps the tooltip upright and inside the shop frame, and dims the other two to the configured alpha.
- The touched relic's caption swaps from its price to its name, and its tooltip lists the effect (with its final number), the formula, and one line per stat including zeros.
- Loaded Dice reads `(base + its own AGI + AGI)%` as its formula and a single resolved percentage as its effect; the two differ by exactly the authored `perStatPoint` times the player's Agility.
- Moving the pointer directly between two relics swaps focus cleanly with no flicker, and a dimmed relic is still clickable and still buys.
- A Strength card (for example Strike) shows its printed damage at rest and the boosted, green, stat-scaled damage on hover; a dynamic card such as Chain Reaction reads the same in both states.
- `Assets/Editor/DeckEditorProbe.cs` H3-H8 still pass, and the `Shop` and `Relics` probes still run clean.
